Questions

What clients usually ask first.

Anything not covered here, call Jared or send a quote request.

What does it cost to use Gold Coast Auto Group?

Our fee is quoted up front and built into the number you see. There are no hidden charges, and the price we quote is the price you sign. [Jared: confirm or adjust wording.]

How long does it take to find a car?

Common builds are often located within days. Rare specs, allocations and off-market vehicles can take a few weeks. We tell you a realistic timeline before we start the search.

Can you take my current car as a trade-in?

Yes. Tell us about it in the quote form and we will value it as part of the deal, or arrange a separate sale if that nets you more.

Do I have to come to Great Neck?

No. Everything from the first call to signing can be handled remotely, and the car is delivered to your door in any state.

Can I lease or finance a car sourced out of state?

Yes. Leasing and financing are available on every vehicle we source, wherever it is found. We handle the paperwork and registration for your home state.

Do you work with exotic and collector cars?

Yes. We source allocations, wholesale and private-collection vehicles for collectors, and can manage the sale of vehicles you already own. Inquiries are handled personally and discreetly.
