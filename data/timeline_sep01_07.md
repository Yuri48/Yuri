OpenAI delayed Astra, paused and restarted frontier RL after the Hugging Face incident, added stricter isolation, checkpoint encryption and tool-use monitoring, and said strongest cyber workflows go first to alpha testers / "Daybreak Blue" defenders. Sources: OpenAI https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/ ; PYMNTS https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-says-new-model-meets-its-critical-cybersecurity-threshold/ ; Quartz (Sept 2) https://qz.com/openai-astra-critical-cybersecurity-threshold-release-090226 ; InfoQ https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/gpt-6-astra-critical-cyber/

Anthropic remains the only major lab absent after its DoD contract dispute (surveillance/autonomous-weapons language) and the court ruling that the blacklisting was unlawful retaliation. Tech Times reported Grok was deployed despite engineers saying CSAM has "no reliable fix."
